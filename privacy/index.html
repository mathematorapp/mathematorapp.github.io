Mathemator – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Mathemator is an app that teaches algebra step by step. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is made by Mathemator (“we”, “us”).

In short: no accounts, no names, no email addresses, no ads, no third-party trackers. Photos you take of exercises never leave your device. The app sends us anonymous usage statistics and error reports so we can make it better.

What we collect

InformationWhat it isWhy
Random install ID A random number the app creates the first time it runs. It is not connected to your name, phone number, or any other device identifier, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. To tell installs apart in statistics, and to decide whether this installation may use the app (for example, during free early access).
Basic app and device information App version, platform (Android or iOS), and the country the connection comes from (worked out from the internet connection, never asked). To keep the app working on every version and to understand where it is used.
Usage statistics Which kind of exercise was started or solved, how many steps and how long it took, which hints, undo, or drag moves were used, and whether the camera recognized an exercise (yes or no). To find the parts of the app that are hard to use and improve them.
Exercises the app could not handle The text of an exercise the app could not read or could not solve. To fix the math engine.
Error and bug reports If the app crashes: the error message, technical details, and the exercise on screen. If you send a bug report: the message you write and the exercise on screen. To find and fix bugs.

What we do not collect

  • Photos. When you scan an exercise with the camera, the picture is read on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or precise location.
  • Advertising IDs or other device identifiers.

Children

Mathemator is meant for students of all ages, including children under 13, who should use it under the supervision of a parent or teacher.

  • We do not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app has no accounts, chat, or social features.
  • The information above is used only to run and improve the app. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold or shared.
  • The bug report box is free text. We ask parents to make sure children do not write personal details there. If that happens, contact us and we will delete it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. It is stored on our own server, which runs on Google Cloud, with the database at Neon, both in the European Union (Frankfurt). These companies only host the data for us. All communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

  • Detailed usage statistics: 90 days, then they are deleted.
  • Anonymous daily totals (for example, “120 exercises were solved on Monday”), which contain no install IDs: kept permanently.
  • Exercises the app could not handle: kept until the math engine is fixed. They are not linked to an install ID.
  • Bug and error reports: kept as long as they help us fix the problem, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the install ID and everything the app saved on your device.
  • You or your parent can ask us to see or delete any information about you, for example a bug report you sent. Write to us at the address below and tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said.
  • If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Payments

Future versions may offer a subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your card details. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
